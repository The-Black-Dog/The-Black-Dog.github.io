Search this website

A search bar inside a website only finds things on that one website, not the whole internet.

If you're on Amazon and search "coffee maker," you only see coffee makers sold on Amazon. You won't see one from Target, or a recipe from a cooking blog.

The search bar here only finds things on this practice website. A search engine like Google looks everywhere on the internet.

        Typing tips

        • Hold Shift and press a letter to make a capital letter.
        • Press Backspace to erase a mistake.
        • The @ sign is on the 2 key. Hold Shift and press 2.
        • The period . is near the bottom right of the letters, next to the comma.
        • Press the long Space bar at the bottom to put a space between words.
